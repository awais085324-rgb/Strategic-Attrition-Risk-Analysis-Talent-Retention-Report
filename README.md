# Strategic-Attrition-Risk-Analysis-Talent-Retention-Report
## Description:
I recently built an Attrition Risk Analysis Dashboard to solve a critical business problem: identifying the "why" behind employee turnover. Using a combination of advanced logic and visualization, I turned a static dataset into a dynamic decision-making tool.

## The Problem:
The organization is experiencing a fluctuating attrition rate, making it difficult for leadership to identify whether turnover is driven by specific demographics, educational backgrounds, or departmental friction. Without a centralized view, HR cannot proactively intervene to retain top talent.

## The Solution: 
I developed an interactive Talent Retention Dashboard that consolidates data for over 1,400 employees. By analyzing attrition through multiple lenses (Gender, Education, Age, and Job Role), the dashboard transforms raw HR data into a risk-assessment tool, allowing for targeted retention strategies.

## Technical Toolkit:

VLOOKUP & IF: To categorize complex employee data and handle logical risk flags.

SUMIF & CONCATENATE: For dynamic data aggregation and creating unique identifier strings for deep-dive analysis.

Pivot Table Analysis: To slice through 1,400+ records and identify trends across departments and age bands.

Interactive Dashboarding: Built with Slicers and Filter Panels to allow stakeholders to perform their own "what-if" analysis.
